Website Planning Document

W05 Project Plan for Global Sanctuary

Site Name

Proposed Name: Global Sanctuary: The Temple Explorer

Why this name?

The name suggests a peaceful, comprehensive guide to sacred spaces worldwide. It emphasizes the "explorer" aspect, encouraging users to discover historical and architectural wonders across different cultures.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide a responsive and interactive gallery of temples from around the world. It will serve as an educational resource for historians and a travel guide for cultural explorers, offering filtering capabilities based on size, age, and location, as well as a community-driven review system.

Scenarios

The target audience for this website will likely ask the following questions when visiting:

  • Scenario 1: Which historical temples in Southeast Asia were built before the 12th century?
  • Scenario 2: I am a student traveler; where can I find a list of "small" or "local" temples that aren't overcrowded by tourists?

Typography

To ensure a highly readable and modern user experience, the following fonts are specified:

  • Heading Font: Montserrat (Sans-Serif) — Used for main headers and section titles to convey authority and modernity.
  • Body Font: Open Sans (Sans-Serif) — Chosen for its high legibility in long paragraph text and technical details.
